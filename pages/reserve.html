    How to reserve a spot

    1. Choose the division you want to hunt in.
    2. Tap an uncolored square on the map.
    3. Review the row and column below the map.
    4. Tap “Reserve this spot.”
    5. Use “Log Deer” when you are finished hunting.

    Log out without clocking out?

    Your reservations will remain saved, but any deer you harvested today will not be recorded until you use “Log Deer.”
